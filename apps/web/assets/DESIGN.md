## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a modern, luxury wedding-planning experience that balances editorial grace with functional SaaS precision. Targeted at contemporary couples, boutique event coordinators, and high-end wedding vendors, the aesthetic conveys tranquility, timelessness, and organized celebration.

The visual style is **Warm Editorial Minimalism**. It strips away the frivolous embellishments typical of traditional bridal tools (such as glitter gradients and overly swirly scripts) in favor of high-fashion magazine art direction: generous whitespace, refined typographic rhythm, quiet tonal layering, and tactile warmth. The emotional objective is to make event planning feel serene, elevated, and deeply personal rather than stressful or mechanical.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in earthen sophistication, rejecting harsh synthetic tones.

- **Primary Accent (`#B66E56`)**: Muted terracotta used for primary conversion actions, key highlights, active timeline milestones, and crucial interactive signals.
- **Secondary Accent (`#7C8977`)**: Muted botanical sage, representing vendor approvals, RSVP confirmations, budget balances, and organic status indicators.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#D19B7F`)**: Soft apricot clay, designated for hover fills, delicate badges, and warm tag backgrounds.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#252723`)**: Deep charcoal espresso used for high-contrast, readable body copy and structural typography, eliminating the harshness of pure black (`#000000`).
- **Surface Canvas**: Warm ivory (`#F8F6F1`) serves as the base window backdrop.
- **Surface Elevation**: Crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) forms the floating card layers, providing structural contrast against the canvas.
- **Structural Outlines (`#E5E1D9`)**: Soft linen beige provides quiet 1px separation lines across grids, table rows, and modular surfaces.

## Typography

The type system achieves harmony between poetic expression and functional utility:

- **Headlines (Playfair Display)**: Evokes the tactile romance of printed wedding invitations and high-end editorial spreads. It is reserved exclusively for page hero headers, narrative modal banners, summary milestones, and card headers.
- **Body and Labels (Inter)**: Handles all operational data, forms, dense guest-list grids, budgets, vendor contracts, and navigation elements. Its neutral geometry ensures optimal micro-legibility and accessible data-density.
- **Casing and Styling**: `label-sm` applies uppercase tracking for metadata badges, status chips, and table headings.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt modular rhythm using a fluid, responsive 12-column grid.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, with section padding and canvas margins set to 40px (`2.5rem`). Content max-width stops at 1360px for focused productivity.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, 20px gutters, with canvas margins adjusted to 24px (`1.5rem`).
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, with canvas margins narrowed to 16px (`1rem`). Sidebars collapse into an off-canvas drawer.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Component padding relies strictly on `space-sm` (8px) for compact elements, `space-md` (16px) for interior card divisions, `space-lg` (24px) for card padding, and `space-xl` (40px) for layout section divisions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through soft surface layering and whispered ambient shadows rather than heavy drop-shadows or stark borders:

- **Surface Tiers**: Base background canvas is warm ivory (`#F8F6F1`). Elevated actionable areas, dashboard panels, and modular blocks use pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Low-Contrast Outlines**: Every white container or floating card is bounded by a hairline 1px border (`#E5E1D9`). This retains structural integrity against the warm backdrop without visual noise.
- **Ambient Shadows**:
  - *Resting Cards*: `0 1px 3px rgba(37, 39, 35, 0.03), 0 4px 12px rgba(37, 39, 35, 0.02)`
  - *Hover / Interactive*: `0 6px 20px rgba(37, 39, 35, 0.06), 0 2px 6px rgba(182, 110, 86, 0.04)`
  - *Dropdowns / Popovers / Modals*: `0 12px 36px rgba(37, 39, 35, 0.09), 0 4px 12px rgba(37, 39, 35, 0.04)`
- Depth communicates elevation without disrupting the calm, tactile paper feel.

## Shapes

The interface embraces tailored architectural curves.

- **Level 2 (Rounded)**: Base interactive elements (inputs, action buttons, select fields, and tooltips) use `0.5rem` (8px) border radius.
- **Containers & Panels (`rounded-lg`)**: Content cards, vendor profile boxes, and budget trackers use `1rem` (16px) corners.
- **Large Modals & Sheets (`rounded-xl`)**: Primary dialogs, drawer menus, and photo moodboards use `1.5rem` (24px) corners.
- **Tags & Status Pills**: Retain a complete pill radius (`9999px`) to visually differentiate status tokens from data containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid muted terracotta (`#B66E56`) fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Height of 42px, radius 8px, padding `0 20px`. Hover transitions to `#A25E47`. Focus shows a 2px offset ring in `#B66E56`.
- **Secondary**: Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) surface, 1px border `#E5E1D9`, `#252723` text. Hover subtle wash of `#F8F6F1` with border shifting to `#B66E56`.
- **Tertiary (Ghost)**: Transparent background, `#B66E56` or `#252723` text with underline or faint background wash on hover.

### Form Inputs & Selects
- 1px border in `#E5E1D9` with `#FFFFFF` background and 8px border radius. 40px base height.
- Placeholder styled in `#8C8E87`. Focus shifts border to `#B66E56` with a faint `rgba(182, 110, 86, 0.12)` ambient glow.
- Labels sit outside the field using `label-md` in `#252723`.

### Cards & Modules
- Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) background, 1px solid `#E5E1D9` border, `1rem` (16px) radius, and `1.5rem` (24px) internal padding.
- Card titles utilize `headline-sm` (Playfair Display) paired with secondary actions aligned to the top-right.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped (`9999px`), 24px height, padding `0 10px`, typography `label-sm`.
- **Confirmed / Paid**: Background `#EDF0EC`, text `#4E5C4B` (sage derivation).
- **Pending / In Review**: Background `#F9F0EB`, text `#9C5B45` (terracotta derivation).
- **Draft**: Background `#F1EFEB`, text `#6E706B`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size 18x18px. Border 1.5px `#D4CFC6` on `#FFFFFF` base. Radius 4px for checkboxes, full circle for radios.
- Checked state fills with `#B66E56` accompanied by a crisp white checkmark or inner dot.

### Specialized Wedding Components
- **Budget Progress Gauge**: Background track in `#E5E1D9`, filled indicator in `#7C8977` for allocated and `#B66E56` for over-budget warnings.
- **Guest List Data Row**: Alternating subtle ivory hover row (`#F8F6F1`), thin 1px `#E5E1D9` border separator, high-density Inter typography with inline tag indicators.
- **Countdown Hero Bar**: Minimal ivory card with Playfair Display numeral tickers accompanied by tracking-spaced label-sm subtitles.
